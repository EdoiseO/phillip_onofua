# Ben 10 #6 pull-out poster

Motion proposal for the Robert Carey and Eric Canete editions. Both use the same portrait insert. The interior pages are sketched to show where the sheet sits. The two plates show the proposed controls and camera framing.

[Desktop frames](desktop.jpg) · [Phone frames](phone.jpg)

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Rest | Opening the comic reveals the quarter-fold in the right-hand page and a **Poster** button. |
| 0–0.24 s | The packet slides up and clear of the open pages. Both folds stay shut. |
| 0.24–0.55 s | The right quarter swings out around the vertical crease until the upper half lies flat. |
| 0.55–0.92 s | The lower half opens around the horizontal crease. The camera settles as the last panel reaches the reading pose. |
| Reading | Hold the portrait until **Put away** or Escape. On a phone the full sheet fits above the control. |
| Put away | Run the same path backward. Fold up, fold across, then slide the packet home. Restore focus to **Poster**. |

Use the sleeve return’s ease-in-out cubic once per movement. Keep the hinge attached as the camera reframes and leave the reading pose still. With reduced motion, switch directly between the seated packet and the full sheet. For the proposal, the poster image is only referenced in catalogue data.
