Receipt Splitter

A proposed mobile app for turning a shared receipt into clear individual totals by assigning items, tax, and tip across a group.

Status
Concept and workflow definition
Primary problem
Shared totals are tedious and easy to calculate incorrectly
Planned experience
Capture, confirm, assign, calculate, and share

01 · Problem

Make a shared bill understandable

A group receipt becomes difficult when people ordered different items, shared some of them, and still need to divide tax and tip fairly.

The product goal is to replace manual calculator work with a flow that every person can review before anyone pays.

02 · Proposed workflow

Keep correction inside the process

Receipt recognition can be imperfect, so the experience should never treat scanned text as unquestionable. People need a clear confirmation step before assigning items.

Shared items also need to support more than one person, while tax, tip, discounts, and rounding remain visible rather than hidden behind a final number.

InputOCR accuracy and manual correction
CalculationShared items, discounts, tax, tip, and rounding
PrivacyMinimize retention of receipt images
OutputClear, shareable totals for each person

03 · Technical direction

Resolve the risky parts before choosing the stack

The architecture is intentionally not presented as finished. The first technical work should test receipt extraction, calculation rules, and privacy requirements.

Those results will determine whether processing belongs on-device or behind a service, and which mobile framework best fits the product.

Milestone 01Test calculations against real receipts
Milestone 02Prototype item confirmation and assignment
Milestone 03Evaluate scanning accuracy on mobile
Milestone 04Build and test the smallest complete flow

04 · Next steps

Turn the concept into evidence

The next milestone is not a polished marketing screen. It is a working calculation model and a small prototype that shows whether people can correct a receipt and assign items without confusion.

Real screenshots and implementation details will replace these planning artifacts as the application is built.

Current status

Receipt Splitter is a work in progress. This page documents the product reasoning and proposed validation plan without presenting the concept as completed software.
